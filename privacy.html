Kitted — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Kitted is a personal gear-checklist app. This page explains what information it collects and how it's used, in plain language.

What we collect

  • Your email address — used only to sign you in via a magic link (no password is ever stored). It is not used for marketing and is not shared with anyone.
  • Your checklist data — the gear lists, checked items, custom tabs, saved trips, and settings you create inside the app. This is the actual content of the app, stored so it's there when you come back.

What we don't collect

  • No location tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties

Where your data lives

Your data is stored using Supabase, a database and authentication provider. It is only ever accessible to you, enforced by security rules on the database itself — no other user of the app can see your data, and nobody manually inspects it.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and everything associated with it at any time, from inside the app: Settings → Delete my account. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or your data, you can reach out through the app itself.
