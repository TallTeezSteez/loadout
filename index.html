☕ Support this project Privacy Policy
Delete your account?

This permanently deletes your account and every bit of data saved to it — your checklist, custom lists, saved trips, everything. There's no way to undo this or get it back.

Reset to default lists?

This clears your checked items, custom lists and categories, renames, and rules — and restores the standard starter lists. Your account itself and saved trip history aren't affected. This can't be undone.

Delete all lists?

This removes every list and category entirely, leaving a completely blank app — no starter lists, nothing checked, no custom lists. You'd build everything back up from scratch with the + button. Your account and saved trip history aren't affected. This can't be undone.
